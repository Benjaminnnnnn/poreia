## Design Context

### Users

Travelers using Poreia as an AI travel agency to quickly generate and refine trip itineraries.

They use the product both before the trip and during the trip itself, so the experience should help them move from a rough idea to a usable, editable plan without friction in short mobile sessions.

### Brand Personality

Warm, sunny, energetic.
The interface should feel welcoming and confident rather than flashy or synthetic.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing itinerary cards as the core visual reference.
Favor soft warm neutrals, restrained coral and teal accents, quiet borders, and simple surfaces over decorative gradients or glossy effects.

Do not lean into AI-chatbot tropes or corporate travel-site visual language.

### Design Principles

1. Keep surfaces calm and legible, with warmth expressed through imagery, pacing, and cues of discovery rather than noise.
2. Reuse the itinerary card language across new UI: rounded corners, pale backgrounds, subtle borders, and soft shadows.
3. Use accent color intentionally for actions and emphasis, not as full-surface decoration.
4. Prioritize alignment, spacing, and readability over visual effects.
5. Avoid AI-chatbot conventions, corporate booking-site patterns, and ornamental gradients, glow, or overlays unless they serve a clear functional purpose.

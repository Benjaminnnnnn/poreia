## Design Context

### Users

Travelers who want to move from a rough trip idea to a usable itinerary quickly, with enough confidence to act on it.

They use Poreia in short sessions before a trip and during the trip itself, so the interface should help them orient fast, refine plans without friction, and feel supported rather than overwhelmed. They access from mobile, tablet, and desktop, all requiring equally refined experiences.

### Brand Personality

Warm, bold, luxurious, inspiring.

The product should feel like a high-end travel planning concierge: emotionally inviting, visually sophisticated, and technically sharp. It should feel confident and contemporary without reading as cold finance software or generic AI novelty. Inspire wanderlust while maintaining practical clarity.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Verified Design System** (implemented in Auth and Home routes):

- **Core palette**: Terracotta/coral (#D97757, #e66a3f) for primary actions and energy, with amber, peach, teal, and sea accents as secondary mood. Warm light canvas (oklch 0.94 0.004) with warm-tinted neutrals, never pure gray.
- **Glassmorphism**: Luminous layered surfaces with backdrop blur, semi-transparent borders, and soft depth. Used intentionally for modals, search inputs, and accent surfaces—never as default everywhere.
- **Typography**: Serif fonts (italic display for dramatic statement: "The world is yours.") paired with clean sans-serif body (Inter). Display font uses clamp() for fluid responsive sizing.
- **Motion**: Framer Motion with Stripe-caliber discipline. Staggered entrance animations (fade + y-translate), smooth state transitions, reduced-motion fallbacks built-in. Animated gradient rings on search inputs. Micro-interactions (button scale on hover/tap).
- **Components**: Custom SearchPromptBar with gradient ring animation and dual-state variants (overlay for hero, refine for inline). Surface component system with glass/card/subtle/muted/dashed variants. Focus rings with color-mix OKLab blending.

Poreia **does not** look like an AI chatbot, default SaaS dashboard, or generic AI slop. It feels like a authored, luxurious travel experience with sense of movement and return.

### Design Principles

1. **Lead with hierarchy & motion**: Every screen has a clear focal point with motion guiding attention. Animate entrance sequences with staggered reveals (hero text, then search card, then prompts).
2. **Glass at scale**: Use transparent layered surfaces sparingly to build depth, but keep layouts disciplined. Never let glassmorphism muddy readability or compete with primary content.
3. **Intentional warmth**: Express energy through coral/terracotta buttons and accents, warm canvas/neutrals, and sunlit gradients. Balance with cooling teal/sea accents to prevent loudness—premium > loud.
4. **Mobile-first responsive**: sm: tablet considerations, lg: desktop expansions. Breakpoints are content-driven (where design breaks), not arbitrary. Full-height sections scale to viewport with clamp().
5. **Accessibility built-in**: Focus rings visible, aria-labels semantic, animations respect prefers-reduced-motion, loading states clear, error messages styled and positioned for visibility.
6. **Authored, distinctive**: Every surface should feel travel-specific and intentional. No repeated generic card grids or chat-first UI patterns. Serif + custom components + color discipline = instantly recognizable Poreia.
